Aircraft: VC-25A, tail **28000**, call sign "Air Force One" while the President is aboard, "SAM 28000" (Special Air Mission) otherwise. Pilot in command: Col. Mark Tillman.

All times below are given as **ET (UTC−4)** with **UTC** alongside. 2001-09-10/11 fell during EDT, so ET = UTC−4.

## Per-leg sections

### Leg 0 — Sept 10: Andrews (ADW) → Jacksonville, FL → Sarasota-Bradenton (SRQ)

The commonly-cited "direct ADW→SRQ" framing in the task brief is **not what the research supports**. Two independent retrospectives (Your Observer's "24 hours in Sarasota," and a direct quote-level read of Sarasota Magazine's "The President in Sarasota") place President Bush at **Justina Road Elementary School in Jacksonville, FL** on the afternoon of Sept 10, for a "Reading First" leadership-forum event running roughly 15:50–16:30 ET, with the presidential party flying on to Sarasota afterward. (CNN's Sept 10, 2001 dated coverage of this trip was found in search results but could **not** be read — WebFetch returned HTTP 451 for it, and a Wayback Machine mirror is unreachable from this environment — so it is *not* cited as a corroborating source here, only as an unread lead.)

**Which Jacksonville airport is unconfirmed.** No source consulted names the specific field. The only comparable data point found — Air Force One's 2009 visit under President Obama, which used Naval Air Station Jacksonville (KNIP) — suggests NAS Jacksonville is the more typical presidential field for this city, but that's an inference from a different administration/year, not a confirmed fact for 2001. Jacksonville International (KJAX) cannot be ruled out. Task 5 should treat the field identity itself as an open question; both fields' coordinates are given below for reference.

**Every Leg 0 wheels-off/wheels-on time is a derived estimate, not a sourced one** — no source found states an exact clock time for the ADW departure, the JAX arrival, the JAX departure, or the SRQ arrival. Each is derived by anchoring to a sourced ground-event or motorcade time and applying an estimated transfer or flight duration (method given per-row in the table above). Confidence, roughly highest to lowest:

1. **SRQ wheels-on** (~17:20–17:40 ET) — derived from the sourced 18:00 ET Colony Resort motorcade arrival minus a sourced "twenty miles" motorcade distance (typical transit ~20–40 min). Single derivation layer; moderate confidence.
2. **JAX wheels-off** (~16:30–17:00 ET) — derived from SRQ wheels-on (already derived) minus an estimated ~40–50 min JAX–SRQ flight time, itself informed by a *sourced* analog (a press charter flying the identical JAX→SRQ route that same evening took "approximately 50 minutes," per Sarasota Magazine — though that flight departed later, at 18:45 ET, trailing the President's own transport). Two derivation layers; lower confidence.
3. **JAX wheels-on** (~15:15–15:35 ET) — derived from the sourced 15:50 ET event-start time minus a typical airport-to-venue motorcade/security lead time (~15–35 min, not sourced to this specific event). Single derivation layer off a solid anchor, but the transfer-duration estimate itself is generic; moderate-low confidence.
4. **ADW wheels-off** (~13:40–14:15 ET) — derived from JAX wheels-on (already derived) minus an estimated ~1h20–1h35 ADW–JAX flight time (~570 nm great-circle). **This is the lowest-confidence figure in the document** — a two-layer derivation chain with no sourced anchor anywhere upstream of it. Task 5 should treat this one as a placeholder needing independent verification (e.g., a presidential daily diary FOIA release) far more than the others.

Bush Presidential Library's "Presidential Daily Diary" and White House pool reports for Sept 10, 2001 turned up the *existence* of the Daily Diary program and its FOIA release process, but not a digitized, publicly accessible Sept 10, 2001 diary page with clock times — see Dead Ends.

### Leg 1 — Sept 11: Sarasota (SRQ) → Barksdale AFB (BAD)

- **Wheels-off:** 09:54–09:57 ET (13:54–13:57Z). The Commission Report gives "airborne at 9:55 A.M."; Your Observer gives 09:54, describing it as "about 50 minutes earlier than scheduled" without stating the scheduled clock time itself; The Aviationist, citing 84th RADES radar data (Commission File RDoD04021430) and pilot Mark Tillman's account (USA Today, Aug 25 2011), gives 09:57 local. A separately-found "10:30 a.m. scheduled departure" figure (from an unnamed secondary aggregation, not traced to a specific primary source) is mathematically inconsistent with "50 minutes early" from a 9:54 actual departure (that's a 36-minute gap, not 50) — see Conflicts #2b. Not resolved; the scheduled clock time is left unstated here rather than asserting an unverified figure.
- **Route/altitude:** Departed without fighter escort. Initial plan was a direct return to Andrews. Around 10:10 the aircraft turned west and climbed to **39,000 ft** (The Aviationist, radar-sourced), running "the length of the Florida panhandle direct to Barksdale," largely over the Gulf of Mexico. One secondary source (History.com) separately states AF1 flew at "45,000 feet" specifically "during the holding pattern phase over the Gulf of Mexico" — i.e., attributing a higher figure to the same leg. This is a real numeric conflict, not just a rounding difference; see Conflicts #8. No source found explicitly describes a documented low-altitude phase before the climb — the brief's note about an "initial northwest/low-altitude" phase isn't corroborated by anything found in this pass.
- **Escort:** None initially. At 11:28 ET, two Texas ANG F-16s ("Cowry 45," 111th Fighter Squadron out of Ellington Field, scrambled ~20 min earlier) joined up roughly 50 miles off the Louisiana coast, shortly before landing. (Escort details noted per brief instruction — out of scope for further modeling.)
- **Wheels-on Barksdale:** ~11:45 ET (15:45Z), per Commission Report and corroborated by multiple secondary sources (11:44–11:45).

### Leg 2 — Sept 11: Barksdale AFB (BAD) → Offutt AFB (OFF)

- **Ground time at Barksdale:** 11:45 → 13:44 = **1h59m (~2h)**. (Recomputed from this document's chosen wheels-on/wheels-off values; an earlier draft of this document stated ~1h45m, which was arithmetically wrong — it matched the *rejected* 13:30 alternate wheels-off time, not the chosen 13:44.) Bush delivered taped (not live) remarks before re-boarding.
- **Wheels-off:** Range of 13:30–13:48 ET across sources (17:30–17:48Z) — see Conflicts. No source independently corroborated the brief's commonly-cited 13:37.
- **Escort:** Three F-16s (one already orbiting, two more launched from Ellington) per The Aviationist.
- **Wheels-on Offutt:** 14:50 ET (18:50Z) — well corroborated (Commission Report, The Aviationist, 911research.wtc7.net all agree).
- **Altitude: GAP — not sourced to this specific leg.** No source found gives a cruise altitude for the Barksdale→Offutt segment specifically. Several general-audience secondary sources (History.com, SimpleFlying's "Tense Flights") report AF1 climbed to "45,000 feet (the 747's service ceiling)" at some point "during the September 11 response," but none pins that figure to this leg rather than to Leg 1's holding pattern (see Leg 1 note above) or Leg 3 (see below). **Reasoned estimate for Task 5:** given continuous fighter escort from shortly after Barksdale departure onward and the day's widely-reported high-altitude posture, FL390–FL450 is a defensible range — the upper half of the VC-25's typical cruise band (roughly FL310–FL410) up to its FL450 service ceiling. Not sourced; use with that caveat.

### Leg 3 — Sept 11: Offutt AFB (OFF) → Andrews AFB (ADW)

- **Ground time at Offutt:** 14:50 → 16:33 = **1h43m**, including SVTC with Cheney/NSC in the STRATCOM bunker starting ~15:15 ET.
- **Wheels-off:** Range of 16:30–16:44 ET across sources (20:30–20:44Z) — see Conflicts. Brief's commonly-cited 16:33 falls within this range but wasn't independently corroborated by a primary source.
- **Escort/fighter escort (out of scope for modeling, noted per brief):** The Aviationist describes an escort buildup to 11 total fighters — four F-15s (Langley) and four F-16s (Andrews) joining initially, plus the three trailing Texas ANG F-16s from the earlier legs, requiring AF1 to fly a 360° orbit to let the Texas ANG jets rejoin formation before continuing to Andrews.
- **Wheels-on Andrews:** Range of 18:30–18:54 ET across sources (22:30–22:54Z) — see Conflicts. The Aviationist's 18:34 is the most granular/best-sourced figure (radar-derived); most general-audience sources round to "about 6:30 p.m."
- **Altitude: 45,000 ft (FL450), reasonably well-supported for this leg specifically.** History.com quotes pilot Mark Tillman describing flying "up to the unusually high altitude of 45,000 feet," and SimpleFlying's "Tense Flights" piece attributes the climb to "45,000 feet (the 747's service ceiling)... along with fighter escorts" — the heaviest, most sustained escort of the day is specifically this leg per The Aviationist's escort-composition account. Not radar-sourced the way Leg 1's 39,000 ft figure is, but the two secondary sources are more specific/consistent in tying it to this leg than to Leg 2.

## Conflicts

1. **Sept 10 routing — direct vs. via Jacksonville.** The brief frames this as an open question ("direct ADW→SRQ or with an intermediate stop"). **Resolved: intermediate stop.** Two independent retrospectives (Your Observer, Sarasota Magazine) place Bush in Jacksonville the afternoon of Sept 10 (Justina Road Elementary, event running ~15:50–16:30 ET) with the presidential party flying on to Sarasota afterward. **Chosen: ADW → JAX → SRQ**, three-leg Sept 10 routing, with all four Leg-0 wheels-off/on times being derived estimates rather than sourced (see Leg 0 section) — this is an open gap, not a source disagreement per se.

1b. **Which Jacksonville field.** Not resolved. NAS Jacksonville (KNIP) is inferred as the more likely field based on a 2009 Obama-era AF1 visit to Jacksonville using KNIP, but that's a different administration/year and not confirmed for 2001. Jacksonville International (KJAX) remains possible. See Leg 0 section for both fields' coordinates.

2. **SRQ wheels-off time (9/11 morning):** sources give 09:54, 09:55, and 09:57 ET. **Chosen: 09:54–09:57 ET range**, consistent with the brief's pre-verification framing; the Commission Report's "9:55 A.M." is treated as the anchor value for curation since it's the most authoritative single source, with The Aviationist's radar-derived 09:57 as the tightest secondary corroboration.

2b. **"50 minutes early" doesn't reconcile with the commonly-cited 10:30 a.m. scheduled departure.** Your Observer states AF1 "lifts off about 50 minutes earlier than scheduled" but never gives the scheduled clock time. A separately-found claim of a 10:30 a.m. scheduled departure (from an unnamed search-engine aggregation, not traced to a specific primary source) is inconsistent with that framing: 9:54 to 10:30 is a 36-minute gap, not 50. If Your Observer's "50 minutes early" is taken at face value with a 9:54 actual departure, the scheduled time would have to be closer to 10:44. **Not resolved** — the scheduled clock time is left unstated in the timeline table rather than asserting either unverified figure. Task 5 should not treat "10:30 a.m. scheduled" as sourced.

3. **Barksdale wheels-off (9/11 early afternoon):** sources split across 13:30 (The Aviationist), 13:44 (911research.wtc7.net, citing Washington Post/NYT), and 13:48 (a secondary account found via search). The brief's commonly-cited 13:37 was not independently found in any source consulted. **Chosen: 13:44 ET (17:44Z)** — 911research.wtc7.net's figure sits mid-range and its stated sourcing (Washington Post/NYT) is closer to a contemporaneous news chain than The Aviationist's narrative reconstruction, which doesn't specifically cite a primary source for this particular wheels-off time (only for the Barksdale landing and Andrews landing). This is a judgment call; Task 5 should feel free to override with 13:37 (brief's value) if a stronger source turns up, since no source in this pass rises to Commission Report-level authority for this specific timestamp.

4. **Offutt wheels-off (9/11 afternoon):** sources split across 16:30 (911research.wtc7.net), 16:36 (a secondary retrospective), and 16:44 (The Aviationist). **Chosen: 16:33 ET (20:33Z)**, i.e., the brief's commonly-cited value, since it falls within the sourced range and no single consulted source has clearly stronger authority than the others for this specific timestamp.

5. **Andrews wheels-on (9/11 evening):** sources split across 18:30 (911research.wtc7.net, rounded "about 6:30 p.m."), 18:34 (The Aviationist, radar-derived — most granular sourcing found), and 18:44/18:54 (other secondary retrospectives, likely conflating landing with White House arrival or Marine One departure). **Chosen: 18:34 ET (22:34Z)** — The Aviationist's figure, because it's explicitly tied to 84th RADES radar data and a detailed escort-composition narrative rather than a rounded recollection.

6. **Field elevations:** see table above — SRQ's commonly-cited 28 ft (from the brief) wasn't corroborated (30 ft is the converged value across four independent sources); the other three fields matched the brief within 0–1 ft.

7. **"3:07 PM" Offutt arrival vs. 2:50 PM landing:** One local retrospective (nonpareilonline.com) states "3:07 P.M. — President Bush arrives at U.S. Strategic Air Command at Offutt Air Force Base," which appears to conflate the AF1 landing time (2:50 PM, well-corroborated by the Commission Report and two other independent sources) with the motorcade's arrival at the STRATCOM headquarters/bunker building a few minutes later. Treated as **not a real conflict** — landing time is 14:50 ET; the STRATCOM-building arrival (a ground-transport event, not an aircraft event) is ~15:07 ET.

8. **Leg 1 altitude — 39,000 ft vs. 45,000 ft.** The Aviationist's radar-sourced figure for the SRQ→BAD cruise is 39,000 ft. History.com separately attributes 45,000 ft specifically to "the holding pattern phase over the Gulf of Mexico" — part of the same leg. **Chosen: 39,000 ft** as the primary cruise figure (radar-sourced, more specific), with 45,000 ft noted as a possible brief, higher excursion during the evasive/holding phase, or possibly an inaccurate generalization repeated across secondary sources (the same 45,000 ft/"service ceiling" phrase recurs, vaguely attributed, across several unrelated secondary articles — see Leg 2/3 altitude notes). Not fully resolved.

9. **Colony Beach Resort motorcade-arrival time — 18:00 vs. 18:15 ET.** Sarasota Magazine's direct, contemporaneous, first-person account states "The President's motorcade arrived at 6 p.m." Your Observer's retrospective states 6:15 p.m. **Chosen: 18:00 ET**, from the more contemporaneous/specific source; the 15-minute gap is used as the basis for the Leg 0 SRQ-wheels-on derivation's uncertainty band rather than picked as a hard conflict to resolve further.
