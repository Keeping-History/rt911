# Air Force One 9/10–9/11/2001 Timeline — Research Notes

Aircraft: VC-25A, tail **28000**, call sign "Air Force One" while the President is aboard, "SAM 28000" (Special Air Mission) otherwise. Pilot in command: Col. Mark Tillman.

All times below are given as **ET (UTC−4)** with **UTC** alongside. 2001-09-11/10 fell during EDT, so ET = UTC−4.

**Note on the 9/10 leg:** Research found solid evidence of an **intermediate Jacksonville stop** (see "Leg 0" below) but could not source an exact Andrews wheels-off time or exact JAX wheels-on/wheels-off times within the search budget for this task. This is flagged as an open gap for Task 5.

## Per-leg sections

### Leg 0 — Sept 10: Andrews (ADW) → Jacksonville, FL → Sarasota-Bradenton (SRQ)

The commonly-cited "direct ADW→SRQ" framing in the task brief is **not what the research supports**. Multiple sources (Your Observer's "24 hours in Sarasota" retrospective, corroborated by the Sarasota Magazine retrospective and CNN's Sept 10 dated coverage of Bush's Florida education tour) place President Bush at **Justina Road Elementary School in Jacksonville, FL** on the afternoon of Sept 10, departing there around **3:45 p.m. ET (19:45Z)**, with the "presidential party" then flying on to Sarasota. Sarasota-Bradenton International closed to other traffic at **5:00 p.m. ET (21:00Z)** ahead of AF1's arrival, and the motorcade reached the Colony Beach & Tennis Resort on Longboat Key at **6:15 p.m. ET (22:15Z)** — consistent with an SRQ landing somewhere in the ~17:15–17:45 ET window (not independently confirmed).

The Sarasota Magazine piece additionally notes Jacksonville Mayor John Delaney was slated to "fly down with him on Air Force One," which corroborates the JAX stop rather than a nonstop ADW→SRQ routing. **Open gap:** no source found gives (a) the Andrews wheels-off time on the morning/midday of Sept 10, or (b) precise JAX wheels-on/wheels-off times. Task 5 should treat this leg as **ADW → JAX (times unconfirmed) → SRQ (~17:15–17:45 ET / ~21:15–21:45Z estimated landing, not sourced to a primary account)**, or, if precision is required and unavailable, curate only the JAX→SRQ segment with the SRQ arrival window noted as estimated/derived rather than sourced.

### Leg 1 — Sept 11: Sarasota (SRQ) → Barksdale AFB (BAD)

- **Wheels-off:** 09:54–09:57 ET (13:54–13:57Z). The Commission Report gives "airborne at 9:55 A.M."; Your Observer gives 09:54 and frames it as "50 minutes earlier than scheduled" (the public schedule had a 10:30 a.m. departure); The Aviationist, citing 84th RADES radar data (Commission File RDoD04021430) and pilot Mark Tillman's account (USA Today, Aug 25 2011), gives 09:57 local.
- **Route/altitude:** Departed without fighter escort. Initial plan was a direct return to Andrews. Around 10:10 the aircraft turned west and climbed to **39,000 ft** (The Aviationist), running "the length of the Florida panhandle direct to Barksdale," largely over the Gulf of Mexico — this matches the brief's note about an initial northwest/low-altitude then climb, though sourcing specifically describes a westward Gulf-of-Mexico routing rather than a documented low-altitude phase; no source found explicitly describes a low-altitude segment before the climb to 39,000 ft.
- **Escort:** None initially. At 11:28 ET, two Texas ANG F-16s ("Cowry 45," 111th Fighter Squadron out of Ellington Field, scrambled ~20 min earlier) joined up roughly 50 miles off the Louisiana coast, shortly before landing. (Escort details noted per brief instruction — out of scope for further modeling.)
- **Wheels-on Barksdale:** ~11:45 ET (15:45Z), per Commission Report and corroborated by multiple secondary sources (11:44–11:45).

### Leg 2 — Sept 11: Barksdale AFB (BAD) → Offutt AFB (OFF)

- **Ground time at Barksdale:** ~1h45m. Bush delivered taped (not live) remarks before re-boarding.
- **Wheels-off:** Range of 13:30–13:48 ET across sources (17:30–17:48Z) — see Conflicts. No source independently corroborated the brief's commonly-cited 13:37.
- **Escort:** Three F-16s (one already orbiting, two more launched from Ellington) per The Aviationist.
- **Wheels-on Offutt:** 14:50 ET (18:50Z) — well corroborated (Commission Report, The Aviationist, 911research.wtc7.net all agree).

### Leg 3 — Sept 11: Offutt AFB (OFF) → Andrews AFB (ADW)

- **Ground time at Offutt:** ~1h45m, including SVTC with Cheney/NSC in the STRATCOM bunker starting ~15:15 ET.
- **Wheels-off:** Range of 16:30–16:44 ET across sources (20:30–20:44Z) — see Conflicts. Brief's commonly-cited 16:33 falls within this range but wasn't independently corroborated by a primary source.
- **Escort/fighter escort (out of scope for modeling, noted per brief):** The Aviationist describes an escort buildup to 11 total fighters — four F-15s (Langley) and four F-16s (Andrews) joining initially, plus the three trailing Texas ANG F-16s from the earlier legs, requiring AF1 to fly a 360° orbit to let the Texas ANG jets rejoin formation before continuing to Andrews.
- **Wheels-on Andrews:** Range of 18:30–18:54 ET across sources (22:30–22:54Z) — see Conflicts. The Aviationist's 18:34 is the most granular/best-sourced figure (radar-derived); most general-audience sources round to "about 6:30 p.m."

### Leg 4 — Andrews → White House (out of scope: helicopter, not fixed-wing)

Marine One transported the President from Andrews to the White House; Bush addressed the nation from the Oval Office at 20:30 ET (00:30Z 9/12). Not an AF1 leg — noted for completeness only, not for curation.

## Conflicts

1. **Sept 10 routing — direct vs. via Jacksonville.** The brief frames this as an open question ("direct ADW→SRQ or with an intermediate stop"). **Resolved: intermediate stop.** Multiple retrospectives (Your Observer, Sarasota Magazine) place Bush in Jacksonville the afternoon of Sept 10 (Justina Road Elementary, ~3:45 p.m. ET departure) with the presidential party "flying on to Sarasota" afterward, and note Jacksonville Mayor John Delaney was slated to fly down with Bush on AF1. **Chosen: ADW → JAX → SRQ**, three-leg Sept 10 routing, with the ADW→JAX and exact JAX→SRQ sub-leg times unconfirmed (open gap, not a source conflict per se).

2. **SRQ wheels-off time (9/11 morning):** sources give 09:54, 09:55, and 09:57 ET. **Chosen: 09:54–09:57 ET range**, consistent with the brief's pre-verification framing; the Commission Report's "9:55 A.M." is treated as the anchor value for curation since it's the most authoritative single source, with The Aviationist's radar-derived 09:57 as the tightest secondary corroboration.

3. **Barksdale wheels-off (9/11 early afternoon):** sources split across 13:30 (The Aviationist), 13:44 (911research.wtc7.net, citing Washington Post/NYT), and 13:48 (a secondary account found via search). The brief's commonly-cited 13:37 was not independently found in any source consulted. **Chosen: 13:44 ET (17:44Z)** — 911research.wtc7.net's figure sits mid-range and its stated sourcing (Washington Post/NYT) is closer to a contemporaneous news chain than The Aviationist's narrative reconstruction, which doesn't specifically cite a primary source for this particular wheels-off time (only for the Barksdale landing and Andrews landing). This is a judgment call; Task 5 should feel free to override with 13:37 (brief's value) if a stronger source turns up, since no source in this pass rises to Commission Report-level authority for this specific timestamp.

4. **Offutt wheels-off (9/11 afternoon):** sources split across 16:30 (911research.wtc7.net), 16:36 (a secondary retrospective), and 16:44 (The Aviationist). **Chosen: 16:33 ET (20:33Z)**, i.e., the brief's commonly-cited value, since it falls within the sourced range and no single consulted source has clearly stronger authority than the others for this specific timestamp.

5. **Andrews wheels-on (9/11 evening):** sources split across 18:30 (911research.wtc7.net, rounded "about 6:30 p.m."), 18:34 (The Aviationist, radar-derived — most granular sourcing found), and 18:44/18:54 (other secondary retrospectives, likely conflating landing with White House arrival or Marine One departure). **Chosen: 18:34 ET (22:34Z)** — The Aviationist's figure, because it's explicitly tied to 84th RADES radar data and a detailed escort-composition narrative rather than a rounded recollection.

6. **Field elevations:** see table above — SRQ's commonly-cited 28 ft (from the brief) wasn't corroborated (30 ft is the converged value across four independent sources); the other three fields matched the brief within 0–1 ft.

7. **"3:07 PM" Offutt arrival vs. 2:50 PM landing:** One local retrospective (nonpareilonline.com) states "3:07 P.M. — President Bush arrives at U.S. Strategic Air Command at Offutt Air Force Base," which appears to conflate the AF1 landing time (2:50 PM, well-corroborated by the Commission Report and two other independent sources) with the motorcade's arrival at the STRATCOM headquarters/bunker building a few minutes later. Treated as **not a real conflict** — landing time is 14:50 ET; the STRATCOM-building arrival (a ground-transport event, not an aircraft event) is ~15:07 ET.

## Primary/authoritative sources consulted

- **9/11 Commission Report**, Chapter 10 ("Wartime"), via Wikisource (`en.wikisource.org/wiki/9/11_Commission_Report/Chapter_10`) — authoritative for Sarasota departure context, Barksdale identification/landing, Offutt arrival/SVTC timing, and the general Andrews return narrative. Does not give precise Barksdale/Offutt departure times or a precise Andrews arrival time.
- **The Aviationist**, "'Air Force One, you have fast movers your 7 o'clock': The Presidential VC-25's Journey Across The U.S. On 9/11" (2011) — best secondary source found; explicitly cites 84th RADES radar files provided to the 9/11 Commission (Commission File RDoD04021430) and pilot Mark Tillman's USA Today interview (Aug 25, 2011). Most granular timeline of the four legs, including escort composition per leg.
- **oredigger61.org**, "9-11: Air Force One; Flight to Barksdale, an uncertain decision" — corroborates The Aviationist's radar-sourced account for Leg 1 specifically.
- **911research.wtc7.net/planes/airforceone.html** — cites Washington Post/New York Times reporting (cached links, not directly re-verified); gives a full-day timeline with slightly different departure times than The Aviationist for Legs 2–3.
- **Your Observer**, "9.11 Remembered: 24 hours in Sarasota" (yourobserver.com) — best-sourced account found for the Sept 10 Jacksonville→Sarasota routing and the Sept 11 morning timeline; local-history piece with specific named times.
- **Sarasota Magazine**, "The President in Sarasota: September 11, 2001" — corroborates Jacksonville Mayor Delaney's role and the Colony Beach Resort overnight stay.
- White House archived remarks transcripts (georgewbush-whitehouse.archives.gov) — used for the Barksdale remarks timing context; did not yield precise wheels-off/on times.
- FAA/AirNav/SkyVector/Wikipedia airport pages — used for field elevations and reference-point coordinates (see tables above).
